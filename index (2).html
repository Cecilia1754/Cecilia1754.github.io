About

Craft as a way to build community

I'm an art events curator and professional crochet artist based in Braamfontein, Johannesburg. My work sits at the intersection of craft, community organising and wellness — I design experiences where people learn a skill, connect with each other, and leave with something they made themselves.

I'm the co-founder of Earth Hand and Fiber, a youth-focused creative organisation that teaches crochet and sustainable fashion skills to underprivileged kids and women, with the aim of turning craft into a source of income and confidence. I also run my own crochet business, Crocheted By Ci. Alongside that, I partner with organisations across Johannesburg — Greenpeace Africa, Sankofa and the NYDA among them — to curate ticketed events, workshops and community days that combine hands-on making with meditation, environmental education and collective care.
